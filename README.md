# E-Commerce Profit Rescue Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce company's sales and profitability data to identify profit leakage, underperforming product categories, and the impact of discounts on business performance.

The objective was not only to visualize data but also to generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Despite generating strong sales revenue, the company faced profitability challenges.

Key questions addressed:

* Which products and categories contribute to losses?
* How do discounts affect profitability?
* Which regions perform best and worst?
* Where are the major profit leakage areas?

## Key Insights

* Technology generated the highest profit margin (~17%).
* Furniture produced significant sales but a low profit margin (~2.5%).
* Tables emerged as the largest loss-making sub-category.
* Higher discounts were associated with lower profitability.
* The West region generated the highest overall profit.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products.
2. Review pricing strategies for Furniture products.
3. Investigate recurring losses from Tables and Bookcases.
4. Increase focus on high-margin Technology products.
5. Replicate successful strategies from top-performing regions.
